    Report¶

    External Economies¶

    Quarterly Report¶

    Chart Variable Explanation
    External GDP gdpw | gdpw_tnd Real GDP level and trend
    External GDP gap 100*(gdpw_gap - 1) % of trend
    External GDP, Q/Q PA apct_gdpw | apct_gdpw_tnd Annualized Q/Q growth and trend
    External CPI, Q/Q PA apct_cpiw Annualized Q/Q CPI growth
    External nominal interest rate 100*iw Nominal interest rate
    External real interest rate 100*riw Real interest rate
    External nominal USD exchange rate ew Local currency per USD
    External real USD exchange rate rew | rew_tnd Real exchange rate and trend

    Annual Report¶

    Chart Variable Explanation
    External GDP yy_gdpw | yy_gdpw_tnd Annual real GDP level and trend (sums)
    External GDP gap 100*(yy_gdpw_gap - 1) % of annual trend
    External GDP, Y/Y yy_pct_gdpw | yy_pct_gdpw_tnd % calendar-year growth vs trend growth
    External CPI, Y/Y yy_pct_cpiw % change of annual average CPI
    External nominal interest rate 100*yy_iw Annual average nominal interest rate
    External real interest rate 100*yy_riw Annual average real interest rate
    External nominal USD exchange rate yy_ew Annual average local currency per USD

    Focus Economies¶

    Quarterly Reports¶

    Real GDP ratios of GDP components

    Shows each real expenditure component as a share of real GDP.

    Chart Variable
    Private consumption to GDP cpr_to_gdp
    Government consumption to GDP cgg_to_gdp
    Fixed capital formation to GDP fcf_to_gdp
    Exports of goods and services to GDP xgs_to_gdp
    Imports of goods and services to GDP mgs_to_gdp
    Change in business inventories to GDP cbi_to_gdp
    Statistical discrepancies to GDP sdx_to_gdp

    Gaps in real GDP components

    Shows % deviation from trend.

    Chart Variable
    GDP 100*(gdp_gap-1)
    Private consumption 100*(cpr_gap-1)
    Government consumption 100*(cgg_gap-1)
    Fixed capital formation 100*(fcf_gap-1)
    Exports of goods and services 100*(xgs_gap-1)
    Imports of goods and services 100*(mgs_gap-1)

    Real GDP components annualised Q/Q growth

    % annualised Q/Q growth, computed as 100*((x/x[-1])^4 - 1).

    Chart Variable
    GDP apct_gdp
    Private consumption apct_cpr
    Government consumption apct_cgg
    Fixed capital formation apct_fcf
    Exports of goods and services apct_xgs
    Imports of goods and services apct_mgs

    Annual sum of real GDP components Y/Y growth

    % growth of a 4-quarter rolling sum.

    Chart Variable
    GDP 100*(ann_gdp/ann_gdp[-4] - 1)
    Private consumption 100*(ann_cpr/ann_cpr[-4] - 1)
    Government consumption 100*(ann_cgg/ann_cgg[-4] - 1)
    Fixed capital formation 100*(ann_fcf/ann_fcf[-4] - 1)
    Exports of goods and services 100*(ann_xgs/ann_xgs[-4] - 1)
    Imports of goods and services 100*(ann_mgs/ann_mgs[-4] - 1)

    Supporting variables

    Chart Variable Explanation
    CPI, Q/Q PA apct_cpi % annualised headline inflation
    Nominal interest rate 100*i Annual interest rate
    Nominal exchange rate (USD) e Local currency per USD (scaled level)
    Real interest rate 100*ri | 100*ri_tnd
    Real exchange rate re | re_tnd
    Real exchange rate, Q/Q PA, annualized apct_re % annualised real exchange rate growth
    Terms of trade tot Export deflator / Import deflator

    External environment variables

    Chart Variable Explanation
    Effective external GDP gap 100*(gdpwx_gap - 1) Trade-weighted foreign demand gap
    Effective external real exchange rate gap 100*(rewx_gap - 1) Real gap vs USD, weighted for other external countries' moves relative to USD (> 0: currency is competitive against trading-partner basket)
    Real USD exchange rate gap 100*(re_gap - 1) Real gap vs USD
    US nominal interest rate 100*iw Annual external interest rate
    US real interest rate 100*riw Annual external real interest rate
    Nominal USD exchange rate e Local currency per USD (scaled level)
    Real USD exchange rate re | re_tnd

    Annual Reports¶

    Real GDP components, Y/Y

    Annual % calendar-year growth (annual sums).

    Chart Variable
    GDP yy_pct_gdp
    Private consumption yy_pct_cpr
    Government consumption yy_pct_cgg
    Fixed capital formation yy_pct_fcf
    Exports of goods and services yy_pct_xgs
    Imports of goods and services yy_pct_mgs

    Gaps in real GDP components

    Annual variable sum over annual trend sum, to show % deviation from trend.

    Chart Variable
    GDP 100*(yy_gdp_gap-1)
    Private consumption 100*(yy_cpr_gap-1)
    Government consumption 100*(yy_cgg_gap-1)
    Fixed capital formation 100*(yy_fcf_gap-1)
    Exports of goods and services 100*(yy_xgs_gap-1)
    Imports of goods and services 100*(yy_mgs_gap-1)

    Real GDP ratios

    Ratio of annual sums to annual GDP.

    Chart Variable
    Private consumption to GDP yy_cpr_to_gdp
    Government consumption to GDP yy_cgg_to_gdp
    Fixed capital formation to GDP yy_fcf_to_gdp
    Exports of goods and services to GDP yy_xgs_to_gdp
    Imports of goods and services to GDP yy_mgs_to_gdp
    Change in business inventories to GDP yy_cbi_to_gdp
    Statistical discrepancies to GDP yy_sdx_to_gdp

    Supporting variables

    Chart Variable Explanation
    CPI, Q/Q PA yy_pct_cpi % change of annual average CPI
    Nominal interest rate 100*yy_i Annual average interest rate
    Nominal exchange rate (USD) yy_e Annual average exchange rate
    Real interest rate 100*yy_ri | 100*yy_ri_tnd Annual average real interest rate and trend
    Real exchange rate yy_re | yy_re_tnd Annual average real exchange rate and trend
    Real exchange rate, Q/Q PA, annualized yy_pct_re Y/Y % change of annual average real exchange rate
    Terms of trade yy_tot Annual average terms of trade

    Note

    CPI, Q/Q PA and Real exchange rate, Q/Q PA, annualized are Y/Y despite the label.
